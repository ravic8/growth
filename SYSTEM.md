# System Design

## North Stars

### Career

Become a Lead AI Data Engineer, AI Platform Engineer, Senior Backend AI Systems
Engineer, or Solutions Architect earning at least INR 45-60 LPA, with USD
45k-200k opportunities included where practical.

### Product

Build Lens into a serious AI-assisted financial research platform and valuable
long-term asset.

### Personal

Reach peak fitness, remain smoke-free, and prepare well for fatherhood.

## Decision Filter

A new idea must materially improve at least one:

- Health
- Job prospects
- Lens
- Quant knowledge
- Network

Passing one condition only makes an idea eligible. It must still beat the
opportunity cost of the current weekly commitments.

## Measurement Hierarchy

Measure from strongest to weakest:

1. **Outcome:** offer, interview advancement, active Lens user, health result.
2. **External signal:** recruiter response, user feedback, benchmark result.
3. **Artifact:** shipped feature, design document, article, backtest, recording.
4. **Demonstration:** mock interview, explanation, solved problem, case study.
5. **Activity:** hours studied, videos watched, pages read.

Activity is useful for diagnosis but should not be mistaken for progress.

## Area Scorecards

### Job Preparation

Weekly leading indicators:

- 5 high-quality, tailored applications.
- 2 resume-defense or architecture stories practiced and recorded.
- 1 system-design exercise with explicit scale numbers and tradeoffs.
- 1-2 DSA problems, focused on screening readiness.
- Meaningful recruiter or engineering-leader outreach.

Lagging indicators:

- Response rate.
- Screening-to-technical conversion.
- Technical-to-final conversion.
- Offer quality.

### Lens

Weekly leading indicators:

- One vertically useful product increment shipped.
- One user or prospective-user learning.
- One explicit product decision based on evidence.

Lagging indicators:

- Active users and retention.
- Research sessions completed.
- User-reported decision value.
- Revenue or willingness to pay.

### Quant Research

Weekly leading indicators:

- One reproducible notebook, experiment, or research memo.
- Assumptions, leakage checks, transaction costs, and failure cases documented.

Lagging indicators:

- Out-of-sample robustness.
- Stability across regimes.
- Risk-adjusted returns after realistic costs.

### Health

Weekly leading indicators:

- 4-5 strength sessions.
- Walking/cardio and mobility.
- Protein, produce, hydration, and sleep consistency.
- Smoke-free days.

Lagging indicators:

- Strength, body composition, energy, sleep quality, and clinical markers.

### Brand & Network

Weekly leading indicators:

- One high-signal public artifact or Lens update.
- Several thoughtful conversations or targeted outreach messages.

Lagging indicators:

- Inbound opportunities.
- Relevant relationships.
- Hiring-manager and founder conversations.

## Improvement Loop

Each weekly review should answer:

1. What created real evidence or external signal?
2. What consumed time without changing an outcome?
3. Which repeated failure points reveal a skill or system gap?
4. What should be doubled down on, redesigned, delegated, or stopped?
5. What are the smallest next experiments that can test the biggest assumptions?

Monthly, compare four-week trends and adjust allocation. Do not change priority
because of one emotionally difficult week; change it when evidence shows the
current strategy is weak.
